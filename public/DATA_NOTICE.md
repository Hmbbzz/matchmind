# Replay information

This is a match replay without broadcast footage. Team names are display identities; the recorded actions, players and match times remain fixed.

Positions may be observed or estimated. Gaps do not establish that a player was inactive. Shot quality is estimated from position; inferred outcomes are not verified saves, accuracy or blocks.

Previous-match comparisons cover three selected earlier matches per team, not a complete recent schedule or season. Player appearances and valid observations can be fewer.

Answers and saved stories keep their original match time and evidence. Saved, exported and shared stories are distinct actions; sharing does not send a message to another person.
